This directory contains figures comparing the observed and best model's frequencies for different modelling approaches. 

This directory contains four subdirectories, representing different modelling approaches. The first part of the name, either *StORM* or *GYRE*, indicates which grid of oscillation computations was used. The GYRE and StORM oscillation codes are broadly similar, but *StORM* includes second order rotation effects such as the rotational shifts of the zonal frequencies due to stellar deformation and asymmetric rotational splitting. The second part of the subdirectories' names indicates how the best model was selected, specifically whether the rotational splitting was included in the chi-square cost function (alongside the zonal frequencies, effective temperature and luminosity). The accompanying paper's results rely on the *StORM\_withSplittingChi2* models. 

Each subdirectory contains a number of figures available in both .png and .pdf format. Each star has two panels, showing the observed signals (top) and the best model's predicted frequency (bottom). The y-axis in the top panel shows the square root of the relative flux amplitude (except in $\beta$\,CMa, whose spectroscopically detected signals are given km\,s$^{-1}$). Unidentified signals are thin, grey lines while identified ones are black and topped with a marker indicating the degree: circles for $l=0$, triangles for $l=1$ and pentagons for $l=2$. In the bottom panel, the colours and y-axis indicate the radial order: dark blue for $n_{pg}=-2$, light blue for $n_{pg}=-1$, purple for $n_{pg}=0$, violet for $n_{pg}=1$, red for $n_{pg}=2$, orange for $n_{pg}=3$, and gold for $n_{pg}=4$.
One figure, *frequency\_fits\_sorted\_validationStars* shows the frequencies of the six validation stars used to test the modelling strategy. The rest, named as *frequency\_fits\_sorted\_{i}* with *i* varying from 0 to 3 are the remaining 30 stars. These are sorted based on the largest difference between an identified observed zonal frequency and the best model's counterpart when modelling with StORM and including the rotational splitting in the cost function (which matches subdirectory *StORM\_withSplittingChi2*). This order is kept consistent in all subdirectories to simplify comparisons. Consequently, the models at the top of *frequency\_fits\_sorted\_0* reproduce the zonal frequencies best and the models at the bottom of *frequency\_fits\_sorted\_3* the worst.
